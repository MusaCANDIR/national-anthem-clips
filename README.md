- Clips from the owner's own recordings: AR, BD, BG, BR, CD, CI, CO, DZ, EG, FR, ID, IN, IQ, IR, KE, LV, MX, NG, NP, PK, RU, TM, TR, US, VE, VN, ZA.
- All other clips: public-domain recordings on Wikimedia Commons (mostly performed by the United States Navy Band, a work of the U.S. federal government).
